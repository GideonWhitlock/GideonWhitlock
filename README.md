# Gideon Whitlock

I create and maintain practical, player-focused add-ons for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. I keep them supported through regular compatibility checks, public changelogs, and feedback from players.

[Browse my add-ons on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/members/gideonwhitlock/projects) · [Source, changelogs and issue reporting](https://github.com/GideonWhitlock/wow-forever-addons)

## Published add-ons

- [Feed Pet: Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/feed-pet-forever) — hunter pet happiness and one-click feeding.
- [Utility Helper: Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/utility-helper) — contextual class utility and emergency actions.
- [Minimap Button: Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/minimap-button-forever) — a movable organiser for add-on minimap buttons.
- [Campfire Stories: Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/campfire-stories) — human-written campfire horror and personal story libraries.
- [Campfire Tooltips: Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/campfire-tooltips) — clear benefits and profession details on camp-object tooltips.
- [Night Watch Torch Helper: Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/night-watch-torch-helper-forever) — a one-click torch reminder for dark nights and bad weather.

## Feedback

Bug reports and suggestions are welcome through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/GideonWhitlock/wow-forever-addons/issues) or the relevant CurseForge project page. Please include the add-on version, WoW: Forever build, reproduction steps, and any redacted error text.
